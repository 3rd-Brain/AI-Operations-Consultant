## Guides

### <Person>

**Role:** <role link>. **Interview:** <first | follow-up to [YYYY-MM-DD](<transcript link>)>

**Settle:**

- <Each gap this person can close, linked to where it lives: an open question, a step with no Why line, an exception with no "how often", an unresolved owner, a missing baseline>

**Before the call:** ask them to have open <the cases, below> and to share their screen and do the work rather than describe it. Ask whether the call may be recorded.

**Cases to walk:**

- <Two recent <records>, one that went smoothly and one that didn't>

**Open with:** we're mapping how the work really happens, work-arounds included. Nothing here is graded.

**Questions:**

1. **The story.** Walk me through <case> from the moment it arrived to when it was done. (Don't interrupt.)
2. **Each step.** At <step>: what starts it? Why does it exist? Who decides? If it stopped tomorrow, who would notice, and when? How long does one take you, start to finish? How long does it sit before you start it, what is it waiting on, and how do you find out it is ready? When you finish, how does the next person find out?
3. **What goes wrong.** <Exception>: how many last week? Who gets pulled in? How long does one take? What does a mistake cost?
4. **The expert.** What would a new hire get wrong here? What do you check that isn't written down? What do you know that nobody else does?
5. **Close.** Who else should we talk to? Is any of this written down? May we follow up? Would you note when each step starts and finishes for a week?
