## Focus

Documenting how the company runs today, from evidence:

- New evidence (a transcript, document, export, or completion report): follow the Evidence intake chain.
- The company itself (profile, tools, data architecture): follow the Company chain.
- Workflows, SOPs, and roles: follow the Workflow Family chain.
- Who to talk to next and what to ask: follow the Interviews chain.

Capture pains as they appear; ranking them is planning work.
