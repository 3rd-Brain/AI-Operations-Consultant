## Focus

From a mapped company to approved, valued Projects. Follow the Planning chain:

- Rank the pains around the one constraint.
- Take one business goal from the sponsor, then draft a Roadmap; candidates are decided one by one.
- Write a Project for each approved candidate, with its future state and the step sort.
- Write its value once the baseline and target are confirmed.

If the workflows the plan rests on are not mapped yet, say so and map them first.
